# Discovery

## Purpose

Collect information from people, documents, systems, and observed work.

## Prepare

- identify the right participants;
- define the session goal;
- prepare open questions and follow-ups;
- list existing evidence;
- choose interview, workshop, observation, survey, or document analysis;
- define how results will be confirmed.

## Conduct

Ask open questions first. Follow up on reasons, rules, exceptions, and examples.
Separate what happens now from what should happen later.

## Confirm

Return the summary to participants. Mark confirmed facts, corrections,
unresolved questions, and new evidence.

## Agent use

The agent can prepare the agenda, question set, summary, glossary, and gap list.
It cannot confirm stakeholder intent.

## Gate G1

Pass when relevant stakeholders, sources, questions, and confirmation method
are known.
