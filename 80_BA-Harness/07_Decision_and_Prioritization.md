# Decisions and prioritization

## Compare options

For each option, record:

- expected value;
- cost or effort;
- risk;
- dependencies;
- reversibility;
- time to learn;
- impact on users and operations.

## Prioritize

Record the chosen criteria before ranking items.
Use a lightweight method that fits the decision.
Do not present an arbitrary ranking as an objective fact.

## Decision record

Include context, options, selected option, reasons, trade-offs, consequences,
owner, date, and conditions for review.

## Agent use

The agent can structure options and expose trade-offs.
People with decision authority confirm the choice.
