# Intake

## Purpose

Turn a vague request into a bounded analysis task.

## Capture

- problem, not a requested feature only;
- business goal;
- expected outcome;
- users and stakeholders;
- scope and exclusions;
- known constraints;
- deadline and decision date;
- available sources;
- open questions.

## Agent output

Ask the agent for:

1. Problem Statement.
2. success outcome;
3. assumptions;
4. unknowns;
5. stakeholder list;
6. clarification questions;
7. proposed analysis plan.

## Gate G0

Pass when the problem, desired result, scope, and next decision are clear.
Do not create detailed requirements before this gate.
