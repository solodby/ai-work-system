# Outcome review

## Compare

- original problem;
- expected outcome;
- delivered result;
- measured result;
- user or stakeholder feedback;
- new risks;
- assumptions that proved wrong.

## Capture learning

Update the relevant context, process, decision, requirement, or incident note.
Record what should change in the next discovery or delivery cycle.

## Gate G5

Pass when the result is measured or explicitly marked as not yet measurable,
and the remaining gap has an owner and next action.
