# Delivery readiness

Before handing work to delivery, check:

- goal and scope are clear;
- user or business value is stated;
- requirements are linked to the goal;
- acceptance criteria are testable;
- normal and exceptional flows are covered;
- dependencies have owners;
- non-functional needs are recorded;
- risks and open questions are visible;
- change impact is understood;
- the delivery team can explain the expected result.

The agent can run a completeness review and prepare a question list.
The analyst owns the final quality of the package.

## Gate G4

Pass when delivery can start without relying on hidden context or unresolved
critical assumptions.
