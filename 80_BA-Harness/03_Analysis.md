# Analysis

## Purpose

Convert collected information into a shared model of the problem and possible
solutions.

## Produce

- current state;
- future state;
- actors and responsibilities;
- process and decision models;
- business rules;
- data and dependencies;
- risks and constraints;
- alternative solution options;
- measurable outcomes.

## Agent review

Ask the agent to find:

- ambiguous terms;
- missing actors;
- conflicting rules;
- missing exception paths;
- unsupported assumptions;
- dependencies without owners;
- outcomes without measures.

## Gate

Pass when the problem, current state, desired state, rules, risks, and options
are understandable to the relevant stakeholders.
