# BA discovery

Prepare or analyze interviews, workshops, observation, surveys, and document
analysis.

Prepare the goal, participants, open questions, follow-ups, evidence,
constraints, and confirmation method.

After the session, separate confirmed facts, corrections, decisions,
assumptions, and open questions.

Read `80_BA-Harness/02_Discovery.md`.
An AI summary is not stakeholder confirmation.
