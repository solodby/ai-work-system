# BA intake

Turn a vague request into a bounded task.

Return:

- problem statement;
- business goal;
- expected outcome;
- scope and exclusions;
- stakeholders;
- constraints;
- sources;
- assumptions;
- open questions;
- discovery plan.

Read `80_BA-Harness/01_Intake.md` and related context first.
Do not draft detailed requirements before the problem and outcome are clear.
