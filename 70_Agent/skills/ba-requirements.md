# BA requirements

Draft, split, classify, or review requirements and user stories.

For each item check actor, action, object, condition, rationale, priority,
dependencies, acceptance criteria, edge cases, source, and linked goal.

Separate confirmed meaning from assumptions.
Do not claim approval that was not recorded.
