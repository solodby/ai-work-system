# BA outcome review

Compare the original problem and expected outcome with the delivered result
and available measures.

Record feedback, failed assumptions, new risks, unmet outcomes, and next
actions. Update the relevant context, process, decision, requirement, or
incident note.

Mark the outcome as measured, not yet measurable, or not achieved.
